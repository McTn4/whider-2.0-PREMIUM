What’s new

New in
WHIDER 2.0.

  • Today, by importance: at the top, a field to write the task, with its importance and “Every day”.
  • Importance in color, kept simple: urgent in red, important in orange, starred in yellow and normal in gray, on each task’s circle, and written next to it (“Urgent”, “Important”).

How do I install it?
Windows
Open the installer. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”: it appears because the app isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet. After that, it updates itself.
macOS
Pick the one for your Mac (Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip Apple” means Apple silicon). Open the file and drag WHIDER to Applications. The first time, open it with right-click → “Open”. It lets you know when there’s a new version.
Linux
AppImage: make it executable (Properties → “Allow executing file as program”, or chmod +x) and open it; it updates itself. On Ubuntu 22.04 or later it needs libfuse2 (sudo apt install libfuse2). The .deb doesn’t: open it with your software installer or run sudo apt install ./WHIDER-linux.deb.

Help

Frequently asked
questions.

Is WHIDER free?

Yes. Every feature, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is it available in English?

Yes, in your browser: the “Open WHIDER” buttons on this page open it in English, and you can switch language any time in Settings → Language. The apps for computers and Android will be in English from their next version.

Do I need an account?

Yes, but you don’t need to create a new one: you sign in with your Google, Microsoft or Apple account. That keeps your tasks safe and gives you the same on your computer, phone and the web: it syncs on its own.

Where is my data stored?

In your cloud account (Google Firebase), where only you can read it, and on your device, so WHIDER works offline. If you lose your phone, you get everything back by signing in on another one. All the details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can keep using it without internet and your changes sync when you’re back online.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not on the App Store yet, but WHIDER works on iPhone and iPad from the browser: in Safari, tap Share and “Add to Home Screen”, and it will open like an app. With your account you get the same as on your computer.

And on Android?

It’s coming soon to Google Play. In the meantime, open it in Chrome and, from the ⋮ menu, choose “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).

Can I bring my tasks from another app?

Yes: from Todoist, Google Tasks, Notion, calendars (.ics) or any spreadsheet (CSV), in Ajustes → Datos → Importar desde otras apps (Settings → Data → Import from other apps). You’ll see a preview and can undo it for a day.

Why does Windows say it “protected your PC”?

Because the installer isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. Every version is published on GitHub with its SHA-256 checksum, so you can check that the file hasn’t been altered.

Are chats encrypted?

Messages are encrypted in transit and stored encrypted on Google’s servers, where only the chat’s members can read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Inside the app
