How do I install it?
Windows
Open the installer. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”: it appears because the app isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet.
macOS
Open the file and drag WHIDER to Applications. The first time, open it with right-click → “Open”.
Linux
Make it executable (Properties → “Allow executing file as program”, or chmod +x) and open it.

Help

Frequently asked
questions.

Is WHIDER free?

Yes. Every feature, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is it available in English?

Not yet: the app is in Spanish for now. This page and the privacy policy are in English, and an English version of the app is planned.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything is stored on your device and nobody else sees it. Create one whenever you like (with your email or Google) to have the same on your computer, phone and the web: it syncs on its own.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and, if you sign in, also in your cloud account (Google Firebase), where only you can read it. Without an account it can’t be recovered if you lose your device, so keep backups: on desktop and Android, WHIDER also makes an automatic backup every day. All the details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can keep using it without internet and, with an account, your changes sync when you’re back online.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not on the App Store yet, but WHIDER works on iPhone and iPad from the browser: in Safari, tap Share and “Add to Home Screen”, and it will open like an app. With your account you get the same as on your computer.

And on Android?

It’s coming soon to Google Play. In the meantime, open it in Chrome and, from the ⋮ menu, choose “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).

Can I bring my tasks from another app?

Yes: from Todoist, Google Tasks, Notion, calendars (.ics) or any spreadsheet (CSV), in Ajustes → Datos → Importar desde otras apps (Settings → Data → Import from other apps). You’ll see a preview and can undo it for a day.

Why does Windows say it “protected your PC”?

Because the installer isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. Every version is published on GitHub with its SHA-256 checksum, so you can check that the file hasn’t been altered.

Are chats encrypted?

Messages are encrypted in transit and stored encrypted on Google’s servers, where only the chat’s members can read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
